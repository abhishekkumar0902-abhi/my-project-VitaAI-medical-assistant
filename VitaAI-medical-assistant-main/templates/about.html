About VitaAI Project

Gen-AI Powered Medical Assistant

description Project Mission

VitaAI is an advanced health-intelligence platform dedicated to making medical knowledge more accessible. By combining Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with large language models like Llama 3 (via Groq), we provide users with evidence-based insights derived directly from verified medical literature. Our mission is to reduce health misinformation by grounding every AI response in expert-reviewed data.

VitaAI supports multilingual interactions, allowing users to consult in Hindi and English for better health understanding.

Access the repository of medical books and research materials used.
